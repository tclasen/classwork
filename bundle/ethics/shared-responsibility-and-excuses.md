# Distributed attribution

Responsibility can be shared when several agents contribute to an action,
outcome, or institutional practice. Shared responsibility does not imply equal
responsibility: assess each agent's knowledge, control, authority, contribution,
alternatives, opportunity to prevent or repair harm, and dependence on others.

An **excuse** is a condition that reduces or removes responsibility without
necessarily showing that the action was good; coercion, incapacity, ignorance,
or lack of control may matter depending on the governing framework. A role or
audit record supplies evidence, but does not mechanically determine moral
blame.[^sep-moral-responsibility]

[Norms, agency, and control](norms-agency-and-control.md) provides the ethical
vocabulary; [roles, authority, and organizations](../foundations/roles-authority-and-organizations.md)
and [information provenance and trust](../information-systems/information-provenance-and-trust.md)
provide technical context without collapsing it into moral judgment.
