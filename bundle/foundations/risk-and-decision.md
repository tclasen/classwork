# Evaluating possible outcomes

A **decision** selects a judgment or response from available alternatives. A
**criterion** is a stated basis for evaluating an alternative. A **threshold**
is a boundary at which the response changes. A **consequence** is an outcome
that matters for the decision. **Risk** is the possibility of a harmful or
otherwise unwanted consequence, considered together with uncertainty and the
decision's context.

Risk is not a property that exists independently of a purpose: the same event
can be high-risk for one asset or stakeholder and low-risk for another. A
defensible decision states the alternatives, assumptions, evidence, possible
outcomes, criteria, and what uncertainty remains. It should distinguish “not
observed” from “does not exist.”

In the [reinforcement-learning feedback loop](../artificial-intelligence/reinforcement-learning-feedback-loop.md),
the policy selects among actions under an objective, while exploration,
discounting, and delayed consequences make the relevant criteria and risks
explicit rather than implicit.
