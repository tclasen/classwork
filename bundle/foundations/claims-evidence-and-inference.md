# Reasoning from support

A **claim** is a proposition presented as true or plausible. **Evidence** is
information relevant to evaluating that proposition. **Inference** is the
reasoning that connects evidence to a conclusion. Evidence does not become
decisive merely because it exists: its relevance, reliability, completeness,
and possible alternatives must be considered.

The basic pattern is: state a claim, identify the evidence, explain the rule
or reasoning that connects them, and state uncertainty or conditions. A record
may be evidence for one claim and merely an object of study for another. The
quality of an inference depends on the [measurement and uncertainty](measurement-and-uncertainty.md)
behind the evidence and the [semantics and models](semantics-and-models.md)
used to interpret it.

and applied to [assurance cases](../assurance/assurance-case.md).
