# References

## Research papers

* [ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models](react-2023.md) - ICLR 2023 paper proposing interleaved language-model reasoning and task-specific actions for interactive problem solving.
* [Attention Is All You Need](attention-is-all-you-need.md) - NeurIPS 2017 paper introducing the Transformer architecture based on attention rather than recurrence or convolution.
* [Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback](instructgpt-2022.md) - NeurIPS 2022 research paper describing supervised instruction tuning and reinforcement learning from human feedback for language models.
* [Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model](dpo-2023.md) - NeurIPS 2023 research paper describing direct preference optimization as a preference-based alternative to explicit reward-model training and reinforcement-learning fine-tuning.

## Standards and guidance

* [Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile](nist-ai-600-1.md) - NIST AI 600-1, a July 2024 profile applying the AI Risk Management Framework to generative artificial intelligence risks and lifecycle activities.
