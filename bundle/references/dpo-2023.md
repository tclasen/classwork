# Publication

Rafael Rafailov, Archit Sharma, Eric Mitchell, Stefano Ermon, Christopher D.
Manning, and Chelsea Finn published this paper at the NeurIPS 2023 Main
Conference Track.[^dpo-neurips]

# Contribution

The paper is the primary source for the DPO comparison in [language-model adaptation stages](../artificial-intelligence/language-model-adaptation-stages.md): it describes a direct preference objective that avoids fitting a
separate reward model and sampling from the language model during fine-tuning
as required by the procedure it compares against.[^dpo-arxiv]
The comparison between direct preference optimization and reward-model-based
RLHF is developed in [preference learning and reward modeling](../artificial-intelligence/preference-learning-and-reward-modeling.md).
